What “temporary” and “persisted” mean

“Persisted” means kept after a browser restart or extension reload. Popup results clear when it closes.

One download or navigation record can stay in memory for up to five minutes, often less if Chrome stops the background process. Navigation also clears when the tab, window or document changes.

The session key and anonymous request counters last until the browser restarts or the extension is reloaded, disabled or updated. The counters contain four numbers: a day, a count and two deadlines. No request log, hostname or report.

VirusTotal and your key

A confirmed lookup sends the hostname and your API key to VirusTotal over HTTPS. No full URL, typed values, page contents, cookies, extension list or files are sent.

VirusTotal may scan the hostname and add it to its shared dataset. Avoid confidential hostnames. A failed or cancelled request may already have sent the hostname and key. PrivacyLens saves no reports.

How PrivacyLens uses this key

Session only is the default. Remember saves only the key locally in your browser profile, without sync. PrivacyLens does not encrypt it as a secret vault; someone with profile access may recover it.

The saved key is never filled back into settings. Saving does not test it or send a request. Forget removes PrivacyLens’s copies; it does not revoke the key at VirusTotal.

The public API is for permitted personal/academic, non-commercial use, with 4 requests per minute and 500 per day. PrivacyLens spaces requests and respects quota errors. Account restrictions and other clients still apply.
